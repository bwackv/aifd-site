01The Brief

You direct. The A.I. executes.

You are a creative director at a marketing/advertising firm. Your client needs a full brand makeover to launch an upcoming ad campaign. You are in a senior position and no longer get your hands dirty in the software suite. Instead, you will use Copilot as your text/image/file/video worker to execute a full-scale brand overhaul plus ad campaign for a major client.

You choose the brand. You develop the campaign. This doesn't need to be an NYC-based brand. It just has to be a product launch for NYC, which means your print and digital campaign assets are NYC-focused. This is broad, so if you're doing an airline, it can be in an NYC airport. Maybe it is a new sneaker for Supreme, a new frame for Warby Parker, a new class of airplane for JetBlue, or a new flavor for Chobani. It is up to you to do the research and choose the brand. Think about what you are into — music, fashion, sports, arts. The more you are interested in the brand, the more engaging this will be.

A.I. is the worker. You are the director. Every asset in this project — brand brief, style guide, logomark, logotype, campaign imagery, mockup environments, experimental piece — should be generated by A.I. under your direction. You are not producing these assets by hand. Your role is to prompt, evaluate, and iterate until the A.I. produces something that meets your direction.

The A.I. will not produce what you want on the first attempt. Expect inconsistent imagery, misread instructions, drifted color palettes, and outputs that miss the brief. Iteration and specificity are how you correct course. Your grade reflects how effectively you direct the tools to produce a coherent brand package.

Some inconsistency across assets is acceptable. The strength of the system you build and the clarity of your direction are what matter.

02Phase 1

Brand Foundation  ·  Due [date]

Learning Goal: Develop a coherent brand identity and a documented work plan, both directed by you and produced through A.I. collaboration.

What you must produce:

1. Work Plan A document outlining the full project, generated and formatted by Copilot under your direction. A website or a PDF — your choice. Should include all deliverables, a detailed style guide breakdown, and step-by-step plans for accomplishing each task.
2. Brand Brief + Style Guide A multi-page document that presents your brand foundation. Format is your choice: a website (HTML/CSS/JS, single file or multi-page) or a PDF. Either way, it must be A.I.-generated under your direction. Must include:
  • Brand brief — a written description of the brand overhaul concept (A.I.-generated)
  • Moodboard — a minimum of twenty images
  • Logomark — the graphical logo, generated with an A.I. image tool. Light editing in Photoshop is acceptable if needed for cleanup.
  • Logotype — the company name set in the specified font family. May be stylized.
  • Font usage guide — what fonts are used and where (e.g., headline font vs. body font)
  • Color guide — primary and any secondary brand colors with hex/RGB codes
  • Consistent visual system across all pages or screens
3. Written Response to "Oy, A.I." by Jaron Lanier A short written response to the essay. See the Reading & Research section for details.

04Phase 3

Experimental Piece + Final Submission  ·  Due [date]

Learning Goal: Push beyond the standard deliverables into something that sets the brand apart. Demonstrate the full arc of A.I. collaboration through documentation.

What you must produce:

1. Experimental Piece One experimental brand asset. Options include an interactive animation, a one-page promotional website, a web-based interactive built in HTML/CSS/JavaScript, or a short motion graphic. This is the most challenging and least predictable deliverable. We will talk about animation techniques in class. Resilience and iteration matter more than polish here.

05Grading Criteria

Graded holistically on consistency.

Weekly work counts toward your final grade. Each phase has a firm due date. Missing a weekly deliverable will drop your project one full grade level. Each additional missed deliverable drops it another grade level. The work is structured to progress week by week and cannot be completed entirely in the final week.

Weight Component What It Measures
25% Brand Foundation (Phase 1) Strength and clarity of the brand concept; quality of the style guide; completeness of the work plan
35% Campaign Production (Phase 2) Visual cohesion across print + digital; mockup quality and execution; consistency with the established brand identity
25% Experimental Piece + Final Outcome (Phase 3) Ambition and execution of the experimental piece; overall power and impact of the full brand package
15% A.I. Direction Quality Evidence of skilled prompting and iteration in the conversation log. Reviewed continuously across all phases.

The package will be graded holistically on consistency. All assets must feel like they belong to the same brand.

07Tips

Specific direction beats general direction.

  • Specific direction produces better results than general direction. "Make it cooler" leaves the A.I. to guess. "Increase the contrast on the typography, shift the palette toward warm tones, and tighten the kerning on the logotype" gives the A.I. something concrete to work with.
  • If the A.I. drifts off-brand, paste your style guide back into the conversation.
  • If a tool produces the same wrong output after three attempts, rephrase the request from scratch or switch tools rather than continuing to retry the same prompt.
  • You may create deliverables beyond what is listed here, or develop new ideas in collaboration with the A.I. Treat this brief as a starting point.
