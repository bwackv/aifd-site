Working with A.I. — Three-Week Brand Overhaul Project

01The Brief

You are a creative director at a marketing/advertising firm. Your client needs a full brand makeover to launch an upcoming ad campaign for a new product/product line launch: a new flavor for Chobani, or a Spring 2027 line for Zara. This means it can be one product, or a product line. You are in a senior position and no longer get your hands dirty in the software suite. Instead, you will use Gemini as your text/image/file/video worker to execute a full-scale brand overhaul plus ad campaign for a major client.

You choose the brand. You develop the campaign. This doesn't need to be an NYC-based brand. It just has to be a product launch for NYC, which means your print and digital campaign assets are NYC-focused. This is broad, so if you're doing an airline, it can be in an NYC airport. Maybe it is a new sneaker for Supreme, a new frame for Warby Parker, a new class of airplane for JetBlue, or a new flavor for Chobani. It is up to you to do the research and choose the brand. Think about what you are into — music, fashion, sports, arts. The more you are interested in the brand, the more engaging this will be.

A.I. is the worker. You are the director. Every asset in this project — brand brief, style guide, logomark, logotype, campaign imagery, mockup environments, experimental piece — should be generated by A.I. under your direction. You are not producing these assets by hand. Your role is to prompt, evaluate, and iterate until the A.I. produces something that meets your direction.

The A.I. will not produce what you want on the first attempt. Expect inconsistent imagery, misread instructions, drifted color palettes, and outputs that miss the brief. Iteration and specificity are how you correct course. Your grade reflects how effectively you direct the tools to produce a coherent brand package.

Some inconsistency across assets is acceptable. The strength of the system you build and the clarity of your direction are what matter.

02Assignment Breakdown

You are creating a comprehensive brand system for this product launch. Here is a list of what you should create. If you have another idea, just run it by me first.

  1. Style guide including moodboard
  2. Logo
  3. Dynamic hero image for your brand page
  4. Prompt Builder + Compositor Tool: You will create a brand-skills.md to create the tool. It builds brand-locked image prompts for Gemini / other image gens (like Weave). [compositor description]
  5. 5 print ads. The ads design themselves and each one is mocked up somewhere in NYC. Can be billboard, subway posters, wheat-pasted posters, guerrilla campaign graffiti, NYC magazine ads, whatever.
  6. 5 digital ads. Min. 2 animated for reels, 3 static, but any combination will do. Mock them up on phones / in phone format (i.e. if it’s an Apple mockup, curve the edges and cut out for the Dynamic Island).
  7. Experimental tool: a brand asset built from a tool of your own creation. Remember how your poster generator tool generated a nearly endless stream of new posters? Well, what can you come up with for this brand campaign? And how will it present itself in the final website deliverable?

All of these assets will be combined into the final presentation, which is a one-page brand launch campaign page.

Everything is due before the start of class Monday, Nov 23.
