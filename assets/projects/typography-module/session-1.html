Session 1: Deconstruction, Typographic Hierarchy & AI Sourcing

Goal: Reverse-engineer the rules of Swiss Style, recognize hierarchical structures, and establish an AI communication framework.

01Module

Designing With AI: Generative Pedagogy & Swiss Typography

This four-session module guides students from researching foundational Swiss International Typographic Style through developing their own programmatic poster-generating web application. The core objective is teaching AI orchestration—how to structure prompts, separate concerns (skills.md, handoff.md), and evaluate generative output against established graphic design rigor.

Sketching your own ideas and having them “upcycled” by ai might be something you leverage. Are the “upcycled” images as useful as the sketches? Do they reinforce one another?

02Overview

5.1 · Mon Sep 28 · online

  • Archival Research & Analysis: Analyze the mathematical grid, sans-serif rigor, and asymmetric compositions of Josef Müller-Brockmann, Wolfgang Weingart, Typographische Monatsblätter, David Rudnick, and Willi Kunz through close analysis.
    • TM Research Archive
    • SFMOMA Willi Kunz
    • MoMA Müller-Brockmann
    • Sgustok Design
    • David Rudnick
    • Tschichold Archive
    • MoMA Weingart
  • Typographic Hierarchy Recognition & AI Sourcing:
    1. Identify the specific typographic DNA of the historical references—x-height, stroke contrast, spatial rhythms, and unitized cadencing.
    2. Upload reference posters to an AI vision model. Prompt it to analyze the typographic hierarchy (H1, H2, body copy ratios) and structural characteristics.
    3. Instruct the AI to cross-reference the Google Fonts library and recommend similar open-source typefaces that match these precise proportions.
  • Typography & Custom Lettering: Download the AI-recommended structurally similar fonts. Alternatively, students can utilize Fontra to build out custom lettering, applying curve harmonization and path rationalization to their own pure-vector letterforms for use in the final project. In this case they would have to also be good at drawing type already! A problem in itself!
  • The Orchestration Protocol: Introduce the two markdown files used to manage the AI. Both are pasted into Gemini at the start of a session, and both are updated as the work goes on:
    • skills.md: The grid system math, the typographic scale, and the explicit rules (e.g., “Left-align text,” “Restrict color palette,” “Apply 12-column grid,” “Enforce modular typographic scale”).
    • handoff.md: Where the project stands, so a new chat can pick it up when the old one loses the thread.
  • Task:
    1. Sketch a paper UI for the poster-generator tool.
    2. Feed reference images, font selections, and the sketch to an AI to rewrite the initial project scope into the two Markdown files.

03In class

100 minutes

Choose one poster from the archives to build a tool around. You are choosing a poster you like in order to build a tool that allows you to design in a similar manner. The processes of engaging with work, deconstructing it, evaluating it, and finding a way to make similar outputs are the core of this exercise.

  • 25 min: Archival research, analysis, and historical lecture on Swiss Style and unitized spacing.
  • 25 min: Typographic Recognition Exercise:
    1. Identify the hierarchy (scale, weight, spatial rhythm) on historical posters.
    2. Write down your analysis of the type, the color, the hierarchy, the kind of grid, the tone, everything you notice about the poster into a stream of consciousness/ natural language text document.
    3. Feed this document to ai to produce a skills.md.
  • 25 min: Introduction to the skills.md / handoff.md Markdown orchestration protocol.
  • 25 min: Paper UI sketching.

04Homework

4–5 hours

  • 1–2 hours: AI-Assisted Type Sourcing:
    1. Upload reference posters to an AI.
    2. Prompt it to analyze the type characteristics and find similar open-source fonts on Google Fonts.
    3. Download and organize these files.
  • 2 hours: Iterating with the AI to finalize the two core Markdown orchestration files. Ensure your skills.md explicitly defines the typographic scale and hierarchy rules you discovered.
  • 1 hour: Creating a local project folder and organizing all downloaded fonts, and inspiration images.
