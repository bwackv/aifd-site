7.1 · Tue Oct 13 · online

  • Find a data source that doesn’t require credentials.
  • Apply your aesthetics to this data stream, in a website. Your aesthetic is the poster generator. The data.
  • Then formalize the data and the aesthetic into a new website that shows this information.
  • Assess for yourself whether this presentation “works” — meaning is the data “Readable”? Does this make sense?
  • You can ask typographic questions — does the typography fit the data? Is the data impacting the type? It goes both ways.
