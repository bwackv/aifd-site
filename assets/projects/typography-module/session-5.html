Design Challenge: Connecting to Live Data Streams

Goal: Use your poster generator as the basis for a tool that connects to a live data stream and visually represents that data.

01Overview

7.1 · Tue Oct 13 · online

You’ve created a poster generator which outputs posters. It follows a set of layout/design/aesthetic principles. Today’s design challenge is to evolve it into a more dynamic tool: using Gemini (or another LLM), connect it to a live data stream and have it generate a website whose various variables (type, color, layout, lines/shapes/images) dynamically reflect that data. Maintain the “generative” aspect of the original poster generator app; that is, it shouldn’t create a single site, but rather maintain the ability to create new layouts by changing parameters/clicking “generate/randomize” button.

NOTE: You are creating a new version of your tool, one that outputs dynamic websites. Do not overwrite your existing poster generator, rather use it as a basis for this new tool.

For example, a city comic poster generator connected to Bluesky’s live stream of posts: every post that mentions the city slams onto the poster as a speech bubble with a POW / BAM / EPIC burst and piles on top of the last; the most-used words in those posts become the eye chart, biggest first; the number of posts per second spins the sunburst; the QR code links to the newest post. The generator’s own controls — layout, color, fonts, randomize — still work while the data keeps arriving.

  • Assess for yourself whether this presentation “works” — meaning is the data “Readable”? Does this make sense?
  • You can ask typographic questions — does the typography fit the data? Is the data impacting the type? It goes both ways.
  • If you’re feeling stuck after reading this, start by chatting with Gemini. Give it the text from this page in the poster generator project chat and say “What do you think about this? What are some ideas?” And go from there.

02Live data streams

These are some options to get you started, but you can find your own.

No credentials required

  • Bluesky Jetstream — every public post as it happens, ~40 per second (WebSocket)
  • Wikipedia edits — every edit worldwide, several per second (EventSource)
  • ISS position — where the space station is, about once a second
  • Citi Bike — bikes and docks free at every station
  • NYC 311 — the latest complaints filed across the city
  • MTA subway alerts — delays and suspensions
  • NOAA tides — water level at the Battery, every 6 minutes
  • Open-Meteo weather — temperature, wind, precipitation for any latitude/longitude
  • Open-Meteo air quality — AQI and PM2.5
  • National Weather Service alerts — active warnings for New York
  • USGS earthquakes — every quake worldwide in the past hour
  • NOAA space weather — geomagnetic storm index
  • Sunrise/sunset — today's light times for any latitude/longitude
  • CoinGecko — live crypto prices
  • Hacker News — current top stories
