A Promotional Poster for a Concert Tour or Movie

Develop an analog sketch as the basis for an image generation project. Additionally, recognize the importance of iteration and incremental image improvement.

01Brief

Project summary.

You’re going to start with a sketch for a promotional movie or concert tour poster. The only text should be the name of the band/movie and, if desired, a tagline/enticing phrase. After you create the sketch, you put that into Gemini with a detailed breakdown of what each element in the sketch should represent. You can see from my example below, the building I specified as “The Beresford” which is a real apartment building in NYC.

Make your sketch detailed and your description of its elements methodical, this will yield best results.

02Process

A walkthrough of my process. You should keep going past what I did.
