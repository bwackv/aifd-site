Session 3: Explore, Critique, Choose

Goal: Use the tool to find new ideas. Then critique and choose.

01Overview

9.1 · Mon Oct 26 · online

  • Explore: Make lots of versions. Look for ideas you didn’t draw.
  • Reference: The MIT Media Lab identity (2011, TheGreenEyl and E Roon Kang). Three shapes and twelve colors made about 40,000 logos, one per person at the lab. All of them read as the same identity.
  • Choose: Up to three different ideas, one per entry. Not three versions of one idea.

02In class

100 minutes

  • 40 min: Make versions with your tool.
  • 40 min: Group critique: Your thumbnails next to your tool versions.
    1. What message do you see?
    2. What feels distinctly Baruch?
    3. What feels distinctly space research?
    4. What image would you remember tomorrow?
    5. What would be lost if this symbol disappeared?
  • 20 min: Choose: Up to three ideas, or mixes of several. Say each idea in one sentence.

03Homework

4–5 hours

  • 4–5 hours: Finish each one by hand, in any medium: pencil, pen and ink, marker, paint, collage, digital illustration. The focus should remain on clarity and communication rather than technical polish.
