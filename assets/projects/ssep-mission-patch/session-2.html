SSEP Mission Patch

Session 2: Build the Tool

Goal: Turn your symbols into a tool, starting from your poster generator.

01Overview

8.2 · Wed Oct 21 · in person

  • Your symbols only: The tool uses your symbols and nothing else. It does not make new images.
  • Your thumbnails are the rules: What goes in the center, on the edge, what repeats. These go in your skills.md.
  • Photo cleanup: The tool turns your photos into clean one-color symbols you can recolor.
  • The patch: Arrange your symbols in a 3.5″ × 3.5″ square.
  • Size check: Show each patch big and small, side by side.
  • Export: PNG, 1000 × 1000 px or larger.

NOTE: This is a new tool. Do not overwrite your poster generator; use it as a starting point.

02In class

100 minutes

  • 15 min: Small group critique: Display your patch. Peers respond:
    • What message do you see?
    • What feels distinctly Baruch?
    • What feels distinctly space research?
    • What image would you remember tomorrow?
    • What would be lost if this symbol disappeared?
  • 25 min: Refinement: Refine your patch using the feedback from critique. This is now the visual basis for your patch generator tool.
  • NOTE: Recall that we did this same process to build our poster generator tool. You’re describing your patch and then feeding that into Gemini in order to build a skills.md.
  • 30 min: Written description: Write down your analysis of the symbols, the shapes, the color, the type, the hierarchy, the composition, the tone, everything you notice about your patch into a stream of consciousness/natural language text document. Go into as much detail as you can about every aspect of the patch that you can think of.
  • 30 min: skills.md: Feed this natural language document, which encompasses everything about the patch that you can observe, both technical and artistic/feeling, to AI along with a photo of the patch itself to produce a skills.md. This skills document will be the basis of the patch generator tool that you will build. Discuss this document with AI so that it knows the purpose of the document.

03Homework

4–5 hours

  • 2 hours: Get it working: photo cleanup, arranging in the square, PNG export.
  • 1 hour: Add the size check.
  • 1–2 hours: Make versions. Save the good ones.
  • Post your patch tool to your index.html.
