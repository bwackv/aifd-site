SSEP Mission Patch

Session 2: Building the Mission Patch Tool

Goal: Use your finalized sketch of a mission patch + additional context to build a skills.md that will direct A.I. in creating a mission patch generator tool.

01Overview

8.2 · Wed Oct 21 · in person

We are following a similar process to the one used to create the poster generator tool. You’ll start by describing your patch in natural language, with as much detail as possible. You then provide the A.I. with as much relevant info as possible. This includes:

  • Your sketch.
  • Your poster generator (indicate that it is for context but not to be replicated).
  • The Session 1 and Session 2 (this page) texts.

For now, refrain from giving it additional patches that you didn’t create.

Remember:

  • The patch: 3.5″ × 3.5″ square.
  • Export: PNG, 1000 × 1000 px or larger.

You don’t need to show each patch big and small side by side (yet).

NOTE: This is a new tool. You are going to give the A.I. your poster generator tool for context only. Do not think of this strictly in terms of the poster generator. You might prompt something like:

“We created a poster generator app over the last few weeks. The mission patch tool we are currently building will be different, but some aspects of the poster generator tool may be useful in the planning process so I am attaching it.”

02In class

100 minutes

  • 15 min: Small group critique: Display your patch. Peers respond:
    • What message do you see?
    • What feels distinctly Baruch?
    • What feels distinctly space research?
    • What image would you remember tomorrow?
    • What would be lost if this symbol disappeared?
  • 25 min: Refinement: Refine your patch using the feedback from critique. This is now the visual basis for your patch generator tool.

NOTE: Recall that we did this same process to build our poster generator tool. You’re describing your patch and then feeding that into Gemini in order to build a skills.md.

  • 30 min: Written description: Write down your analysis of the shapes, the color, the type, the hierarchy, the composition, the tone, everything you notice about your patch into a stream of consciousness/natural language text document. Go into as much detail as you can about every aspect of the patch that you can think of.
  • 30 min: Build your skills.md: Feed this natural language document, which encompasses everything about the patch that you can observe, both technical and artistic/feeling, to AI along with a photo of the patch itself to produce a skills.md. This skills document will be the basis of the patch generator tool that you will build. Discuss this document with AI so that it knows the purpose of the document.

    Things to feed the AI in the prompt for building the skills.md:

    • Your sketch
    • Your description
    • The text of Session 1 and Session 2 (the page you’re on)
    • Your poster generator app

    Be sure to tell the AI:

    “We are building a mission patch generator tool. The poster generator is something we built earlier. I’m giving it to you only as context to help build the skills.md, not as something to copy.”

    Be explicit:

    • The final output is 3.5″ × 3.5″, every time.
    • It answers one question: What message would Baruch send into space?

    Things to consider:

    • How does it incorporate text?
    • What font(s) are you working with?
    • How do you define the “grid” or “layout” rules of this mission patch generator?
    • What is your color palette?

    For these and any other questions you think of, consider how the tool generates endless variations on your initial conditions.

    NOTE: Do not have AI write your artist statement.

  • Build the mission patch generator tool: Now read over the skills.md file, refine it (with A.I.), and when you are satisfied with it, tell the A.I. to build the first version of the mission patch generator tool.

    “Based on this skills.md, build out version 1 of the mission patch generator tool.”

03Homework

4–5 hours

  • 2 hours: Get it working: photo cleanup, arranging in the square, PNG export.
  • 1 hour: Add the size check.
  • 1–2 hours: Make versions. Save the good ones.
  • Post your patch tool to your index.html.
