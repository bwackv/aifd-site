05Prompting

Add the setting.

What is the atmosphere like? What else is in the room? What time of day is it?

“a _____ chair [sitting? floating? ominously standing?] in the middle of a _____ room, _____, _____, _____.” 

a chrome chair floating in the middle of a dilapidated school room in the middle of the day. There are no other objects. A wooden floor. Old painted walls with windows up high.

Image · 4:3

06Prompting

Add the style.

If you want something that isn’t photorealistic, what is it? A woodcut, in the style of Picasso, a collaboration between Yayoi Kusama and Chanel.

“a chrome chair floating in the middle of a dilapidated school room in the middle of the day. There are no other objects. A wooden floor. Old painted walls with windows up high. made in the style of a painting collaboration between Pablo Picasso and Jenny Saville.”

Image · 16:9

07Prompting

Take control of the camera.

Angle, distance, crop, lens, and aspect ratio. Shot from above, three-quarter view, wide, close-up, from across the room. 16:9, 1:1, 4:5, 9:16.

"a chrome chair floating in the middle of a dilapidated school room in the middle of the day. There are no other objects. A wooden floor. Old painted walls with windows up high. Shot from slightly above and across the room, on a black and white film camera with a low aperture. 16:9 format. "

Image · 4:3

08Prompting

Give it a reference.

A sketch, a source photo, an image you like. Words are not your only input.

Draw on your chair image, upload it: “use my drawing on this image. put a window in the wall where I circled and take out what I crossed out. keep the chair and the room the same.”

Image · 1:1

09Prompting

Set up a feedback loop.

Hand your prompt to a different a.i. and ask it to rewrite it for an image generator.

In a different a.i.: “rewrite this prompt for an image generator: [paste your prompt]”

Image · 16:9

10Prompting

Don’t stop at a single pass.

It’s usually not perfect the first time, and you may need to start a new chat to get new results.

“a chrome chair floating in the middle of a dilapidated school room in the middle of the day. There are no other objects. A wooden floor. Old painted walls with windows up high. Shot from slightly above and across the room, on a black and white film camera with a low aperture. 16:9 format. The chair is higher off the floor and the light through the windows is harder.”
