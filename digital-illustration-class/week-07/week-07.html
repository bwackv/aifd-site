03Logo systems discussion

Introductory definitions to get us going before we move into the design challenge.

  • Logo system: related marks (logo, monogram, icon, wordmark, variants). Continuity exists between all the related marks.
    • What is the relationship between a style guide and a logo system?
  • Reduction: stripping a mark to its fewest essential forms so it holds at small size and in one color.
  • Mark-making: lines, shapes and textures whose gesture carries character.
  • Keyword generation: listing words to set the concept before drawing.
    • Have you ever made a mind map?
  • Abstraction: translating an idea or aspect of the company/brand into something non-literal.
  • Initials (monogram): a mark built from your letters.
  • Negative space: the empty area around and within a form, used to make a second shape.
