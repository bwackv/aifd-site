Design Challenge: Explore Your Personal Logo

Personal Logo Exploration/Ideation Challenge (75 minutes)

01Logorama

▶ Watch on YouTube

Logorama builds a world made entirely of existing (corporate) logos. Did it make you consider how much of our visual culture is shaped by corporate branding and imagery? Or perhaps how context shapes our feelings of a given brand? And what did nature have to say to this logoscape?

This leads me to ask you a question: if you were to participate in this visual world of logos (and natural disaster), how would your logo look? For this assignment, you’re going to explore that question.

02Step 1

5 minutes

Write 10 words about yourself

Fast list. Don’t overthink.

Examples: calm, energetic, geometric, chaotic, precise, bold, soft, introverted, loud, playful.

03Step 2

10 minutes

Turn 3 of those words into simple shapes

Pick 3 of the words you wrote down. Represent each one as a simple symbol or shape.

Examples: “Calm” → smooth circle · “Energetic” → zig-zag · “Precise” → square · “Playful” → wiggly line

These are NOT logos — just abstract shapes based on your personality words.

04Step 3

15 minutes

Draw your initials in 6 different ways

Try: stacked, merged, overlapping, broken apart, mirrored, rotated, or carved into negative space (one letter formed by the empty space around or inside another). Black and white only.

Goal: Explore multiple ways your initials could become symbols.

05Step 4

30 minutes

Combine shapes + initials → create 6 rough logo sketches

Each sketch = 1 initials idea + 1 shape idea combined. It doesn’t have to be an exact match to the initial idea or shape, but don’t stray too far.

Examples: a circle form containing your initials · initials broken into geometric pieces from your shapes · a symbol sitting next to a monogram · initials stylized to match the energy of your shapes (sharp, rounded, messy)

Fast. Rough. No polishing.

If you find yourself with extra time, do 4 more for a total of ten.

06Step 5

15 minutes

Pick your strongest direction

Circle it.

Reduce it: redraw your mark at thumbnail size (about 1 inch) using the fewest possible shapes, removing any detail that disappears when small.

Write 3 sentences:

  1. Why it works visually
  2. How it represents you
  3. How you would refine it next

07Constraints

  • Black and white
  • Original (not based on an existing brand)
  • Must be readable at very small size

08Deliverables

  • 6 rough logo sketches
  • 1 sentence per sketch explaining the idea
  • Final direction, its reduced thumbnail version, and a 3-sentence rationale

These sketches are a starting point for Project 2: Personal Logo. They do not count toward the 30.
